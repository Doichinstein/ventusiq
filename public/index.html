VENTUSIQ / COMMUNITY EDITION

Open weather intelligence.

A free, open-source view of US weather alerts, built on VentusIQ's daemon infrastructure. Explore what is happening, where it applies, and what has changed.

Official data, visible provenance

Alerts come from the National Weather Service. Areas use the alert's own polygon when provided; otherwise we load its official affected-zone boundaries. Zone boundaries describe the alert's coverage, not an observed storm footprint.

Know how fresh your view is

We check the active alert feed about every 60 seconds. The status shows when collection last succeeded. During an outage, the last available alerts remain visible and are marked delayed. Alert issue time and collection time are different.

Alerts can describe current or upcoming conditions. Always follow official NWS and local emergency instructions. This independent project is not affiliated with NOAA or NWS.

Use it. Build on it.

The original code is licensed under Apache-2.0, including commercial reuse under its terms. Leaflet, Geist fonts, and map data retain their own licenses. The self-hosted Python daemon adapts VentusIQ's collector and freshness infrastructure.
